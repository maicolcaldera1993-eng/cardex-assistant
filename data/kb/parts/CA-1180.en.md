## Fitting {#montaggio}

Only with the boiler drained and cold. Remove the rear panel, disconnect the wires (note their positions), undo the 4 flange nuts, pull the element, always replace gasket CA-1220. Tighten crosswise. Fill the boiler before powering up.

## Service notes {#note}

Check the rating plate voltage before shipping: CA-1180 is 230 V single-phase, CA-1181 is 110 V, CA-1182 is 400 V three-phase. They look alike. Does NOT fit the Marea 2 Evo: different flange, use CA-1185. Fitting with service support, never alone.
