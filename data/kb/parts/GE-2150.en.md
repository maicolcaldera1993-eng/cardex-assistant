## Fitting {#montaggio}

Undo the M6 centre screw, remove the screen, clean the seat, fit the new one convex side down. Do not exceed 2 Nm.

## Service notes {#note}

If the holes are intact it can be saved: 20 minutes in hot descaler, brush, rinse. Replace if domed or with worn-open holes. 70% of requests come from shops that skip the evening backflush with the blind filter: always suggest CR-6052.
