## Fitting {#montaggio}

Unscrew with a 14 mm spanner from the top of the boiler, fit the new one with PTFE tape on the thread.

## Service notes {#note}

A hiss at start-up lasting more than 5 minutes, or a drop on the valve: first remove and clean the pin, then replace the o-ring CA-1261, then the valve. The customer can do it alone.
