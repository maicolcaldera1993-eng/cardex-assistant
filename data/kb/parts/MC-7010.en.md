## Fitting {#montaggio}

Empty the hopper, open the collar until the upper burr carrier comes off, undo the 3 screws per burr, clean the seats, fit the new burrs, zero the collar at contact and reopen half a turn.

## Service notes {#note}

Life 600-800 kg of coffee. Signs: slow grinding, burnt-tasting coffee, warm grounds. The customer changes them alone with the sheet. Same on Monda 65 and Monda 65 Digit.
