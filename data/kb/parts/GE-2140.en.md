## Fitting {#montaggio}

Remove the shower screen (centre screw), lever the old gasket out with a flat screwdriver from the rim, clean the seat of scale, fit the new one with the chamfered side towards the group. Refit screen and screw. The portafilter should lock at the centre within 5-10 coffees.

## Service notes {#note}

Best-selling part. Typical life 6-12 months in a bar doing 200 coffees a day. If the portafilter still locks past 6 o'clock with a new gasket, the portafilter rim is worn (GE-2180). Never put silicone grease on the seat: the gasket slips and bypasses.
