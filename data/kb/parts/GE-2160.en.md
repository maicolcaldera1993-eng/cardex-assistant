## Fitting {#montaggio}

Close the water, release the pressure, disconnect the three fittings (inlet, group, discharge) and the two coil wires. Fit the new valve respecting the arrow. 17 mm spanner, access from the side panel.

## Service notes {#note}

If the valve clicks but the group spits at the end of the shot it is almost always dirty, not broken: open the body (GE-2165) and clean the plunger. If the coil does not click: EL-3085 first.
