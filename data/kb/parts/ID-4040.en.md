## Fitting {#montaggio}

On the water inlet, after the filter. Two fittings and the coil. Respect the flow direction.

## Service notes {#note}

Clicks but does not fill: internal mesh blocked by scale, cleanable. No click: coil (EL-3085) or board. Fills all the time: probe CA-1230 or valve stuck open.
