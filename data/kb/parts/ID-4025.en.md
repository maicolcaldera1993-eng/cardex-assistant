*Valvola di espansione 12 bar regolabile*

_Static sheet. Current stock, price and delivery times: see the ERP._

|---|---|

## Compatibility {#compatibilita}

- Marea 2: 1 per machine
- Marea 2 Plus: 1 per machine
- Marea 2 Evo: 1 per machine
- Giglio 1: 1 per machine
- Giglio 1 Plus: 1 per machine

## Fitting {#montaggio}

On the pump delivery line. Unscrew, replace, set to 12 bar.

## Service notes {#note}

Constant discharge while brewing: expansion valve. Discharge with the machine idle: check valve ID-4030 or fill valve ID-4040. Tell them apart by asking WHEN it drips.
