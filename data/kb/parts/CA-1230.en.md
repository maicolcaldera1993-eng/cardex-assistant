## Fitting {#montaggio}

Unscrew the probe from the top of the boiler with a 13 mm spanner, replace with the new insulator (CA-1232), do not overtighten: a quarter turn past contact.

## Service notes {#note}

Before replacing: clean the tip with fine abrasive paper. In 60% of 'not filling' or 'always filling' calls it is scale on the probe, not the probe. If the insulator is cracked the probe always reads water and the boiler never fills.
