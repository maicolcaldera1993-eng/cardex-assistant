## Fitting {#montaggio}

With service support. Separate the pump from the motor (clamp and coupling ID-4017), disconnect suction and delivery, fit the new pump respecting the rotation arrow stamped on the body. Set the bypass to 9 bar with the blind filter.

## Service notes {#note}

Metallic noise and pressure that will not go past 6 bar: pump. Hum and zero pressure: motor or capacitor ID-4019. A pump worn by hard water shows at the coupling: black dust.
