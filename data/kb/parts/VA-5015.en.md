## Compatibility {#compatibilita}

## Fitting {#montaggio}

Close the tap, unscrew the knob and the gland, pull the stem, replace the two seals and the o-ring, refit without overtightening the gland.

## Service notes {#note}

The customer does it alone in 10 minutes. If it still drips afterwards, the stem is scored (VA-5016).
