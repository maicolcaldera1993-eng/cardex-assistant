# Weak steam, or wand dripping with the tap closed

*Vapore debole, o lancia che gocciola con il rubinetto chiuso*

Known-defects file, Marea family. Internal service document.

## How customers describe it {#descrizioni}

«vapore debole», «poco vapore», «non monta il latte», «la lancia gocciola», «perde dalla lancia», «esce acqua dalla lancia», «weak steam», «not enough steam», «cannot steam milk», «wand drips», «steam wand leaks», «water from the wand», «steam tap leaks», «non esce vapore»

## Procedure {#procedura}

### 1. Ask: Is the problem weak steam, or dripping with the tap closed? {#passo-1}

*Il problema è vapore debole, o gocciola a rubinetto chiuso?*

- Weak steam → go to step 2
- Drips from the wand → **part, fitted by the customer**: VA-5015 (Steam valve seal kit (seals + o-rings)), VA-5070 (Complete steam service kit (seals, stem, joint o-rings), Marea/Giglio)
- Leaks at the joint → **part, fitted by the customer**: VA-5028 (Wand joint O-ring (kit of 10))

### 2. Ask: Is the boiler needle at 1.1-1.2 bar? {#passo-2}

*La lancetta della caldaia è a 1,1-1,2 bar?*

- Yes → go to step 3
- Below 1 bar → **part, fitted by the customer**: CA-1240 (Pressurestat 0.5–1.4 bar)

### 3. Have them do: Have them unscrew the steam tip: 30 minutes in hot water with a tablet, pin through the holes, refit. {#passo-3}

*Far svitare il terminale della lancia: 30 minuti in acqua calda con pastiglia, spillo nei fori, rimontare.*

- Fixed → **fixed remotely**
- Holes worn open → **part, fitted by the customer**: VA-5025 (Steam tip 2-hole)
- Still weak → **part with service support**: VA-5010 (Steam valve complete with knob, Marea/Giglio)
