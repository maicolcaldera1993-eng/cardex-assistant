# The grinder is noisy or vibrates

*Il macinacaffè è rumoroso o vibra*

Known-defects file, Monda family. Internal service document.

## How customers describe it {#descrizioni}

«fa rumore», «vibra», «cammina sul banco», «rumore metallico», «fischia», «noisy grinder», «vibrates», «walks on the counter», «metallic noise», «whistling noise»

## Procedure {#procedura}

### 1. Ask: Does it vibrate and move on the counter, whistle from the motor, or make a metallic noise from the burrs? {#passo-1}

*Vibra e si sposta sul banco, fischia dal motore, o fa un rumore metallico dalle macine?*

- Vibrates and moves → **part, fitted by the customer**: MC-7080 (Anti-vibration rubber foot (kit of 4))
- Whistles from the motor → **part with service support**: MC-7026 (Motor cooling fan)
- Metallic noise → go to step 2

### 2. Have them do: Stop it at once. Remove the hopper and collar and check that the burr screws are tight and the burrs intact. {#passo-2}

*Fermarlo subito. Togliere tramoggia e ghiera e controllare che le viti delle macine siano strette e le macine intere.*

- Loose screws, tightened: fixed → **fixed remotely**
- Screws stripped or missing → **part, fitted by the customer**: MC-7011 (Burr fixing screws M4 (kit of 6))
- Burrs chipped → **part, fitted by the customer**: MC-7010 (Pair of flat burrs 65 mm hardened steel), MC-7011 (Burr fixing screws M4 (kit of 6))
