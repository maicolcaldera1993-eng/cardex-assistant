# Does not heat, stays cold, boiler pressure at zero

*Non scalda, resta fredda, pressione caldaia a zero*

Known-defects file, Marea family. Internal service document.

## How customers describe it {#descrizioni}

«non scalda», «resta fredda», «è fredda», «non va in pressione», «pressione a zero», «niente vapore e niente caffè caldo», «does not heat», «not heating», «stays cold», «no pressure», «cold machine», «no steam at all», «gauge at zero», «non si scalda»

## Procedure {#procedura}

### 1. Ask: Are the lights and touchpad on? Has the boiler filled (no level alarm)? {#passo-1}

*Le spie e la pulsantiera sono accese? La caldaia si è riempita (nessun allarme livello)?*

- Everything off → **technician**
- Level alarm → **fixed remotely**
- On, boiler full, cold → go to step 2

### 2. Have them do: With the machine off, behind the rear panel there is a small red button on the boiler body: have them press it. Switch on and wait 10 minutes. {#passo-2}

*A macchina spenta, dietro il pannello posteriore c'è un pulsantino rosso sul corpo caldaia: farlo premere. Riaccendere e attendere 10 minuti.*

> Ask whether the boiler had run dry: that is why the thermostat trips.

- Heats: fixed → **fixed remotely**
- Still cold → go to step 3
- Cannot find the button → **part with service support**: CA-1270 (Safety thermostat 165 °C manual reset, Marea/Giglio)

### 3. Ask: At power-on, do you hear the contactor click behind the panel? {#passo-3}

*All'accensione si sente il click del teleruttore dietro il pannello?*

- Clicks, but no heat → go to step 4
- No click → go to step 5

### 4. Ask: What voltage is the machine (rating plate): 230 V, 110 V or 400 V three-phase? Which model and year? {#passo-4}

*Che tensione ha la macchina (targhetta): 230 V, 110 V o 400 V trifase? Che modello e anno?*

> Burnt-out element. Order the right version for voltage and model, with the gasket. Evo has a different flange.

- Marea 2 / Plus, 230 V → **part with service support**: CA-1180 (Boiler heating element 3000 W 230 V, Marea 2 / 2 Plus), CA-1220 (Heating element flange gasket, Marea/Giglio)
- Marea 2 / Plus, 110 V → **part with service support**: CA-1181 (Boiler heating element 3000 W 110 V, Marea 2 / 2 Plus), CA-1220 (Heating element flange gasket, Marea/Giglio)
- Marea 2 / Plus, 400 V → **part with service support**: CA-1182 (Boiler heating element 4500 W 400 V three-phase, Marea 2 / 2 Plus), CA-1220 (Heating element flange gasket, Marea/Giglio)
- Marea 2 Evo → **part with service support**: CA-1185 (Boiler heating element 3500 W 230 V, Marea 2 Evo), CA-1220 (Heating element flange gasket, Marea/Giglio)
- Giglio → **part with service support**: CA-1190 (Boiler heating element 1800 W 230 V, Giglio), CA-1220 (Heating element flange gasket, Marea/Giglio)

### 5. Have them do: Have them turn the pressurestat screw a quarter turn clockwise and switch on again. {#passo-5}

*Far girare la vite del pressostato di un quarto di giro in senso orario e riaccendere.*

- Now it heats → **fixed remotely**
- No change → **part with service support**: EL-3050 (Heating element contactor 25 A 230 V coil), CA-1240 (Pressurestat 0.5–1.4 bar)
