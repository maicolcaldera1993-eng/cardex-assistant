# The grinder does not switch on or does not start

*Il macinacaffè non si accende o non parte*

Known-defects file, Monda family. Internal service document.

## How customers describe it {#descrizioni}

«non si accende», «non parte», «macinino spento», «display spento», «non macina con la forcella», «does not switch on», «will not start», «grinder dead», «display off», «does not grind with the portafilter»

## Procedure {#procedura}

### 1. Ask: Which model is it, and what do you see: lamp or display on? {#passo-1}

*Che modello è, e cosa si vede: spia o display accesi?*

- Monda 65, lamp off → go to step 2
- Monda 65, lamp on but the motor does not start → **part, fitted by the customer**: MC-7070 (Double-pole switch with lamp, Monda 65)
- Monda 65 Digit, display off → go to step 3
- Digit, display on but does not start with the portafilter → **part, fitted by the customer**: MC-7062 (Fork microswitch, Monda 65 Digit)

### 2. Have them do: Have them check the fuse under the base (screw-in holder) and replace it if blown, 10 A slow-blow. {#passo-2}

*Far controllare il fusibile sotto la base (portafusibile a vite) e sostituirlo se bruciato, 10 A ritardato.*

- New fuse, it starts → **fixed remotely**
- Fuse intact → **part, fitted by the customer**: MC-7070 (Double-pole switch with lamp, Monda 65)
- Blows again → **technician**

### 3. Have them do: Have them check the fuse under the base and replace it if blown, 10 A slow-blow. {#passo-3}

*Far controllare il fusibile sotto la base e sostituirlo se bruciato, 10 A ritardato.*

- New fuse, it starts → **fixed remotely**
- Fuse intact, display off → **part with service support**: MC-7060 (Timed dosing electronic board, Monda 65 Digit)
- Blows again → **technician**
