# The beans do not drop from the hopper

*I chicchi non scendono dalla tramoggia*

Known-defects file, Monda family. Internal service document.

## How customers describe it {#descrizioni}

«i chicchi non scendono», «gira a vuoto», «la tramoggia non scarica», «saracinesca bloccata», «beans not dropping», «grinds nothing», «hopper not feeding», «shutter stuck»

## Procedure {#procedura}

### 1. Ask: Is the shutter under the hopper fully open and sliding freely? {#passo-1}

*La saracinesca sotto la tramoggia è aperta del tutto e scorre bene?*

- It was closed: opened, now feeds → **fixed remotely**
- Broken or does not slide → **part, fitted by the customer**: MC-7041 (Hopper shutter slide)
- Open, but the beans do not drop → go to step 2

### 2. Ask: Is the hopper cracked, or are the beans oily and bridging? {#passo-2}

*La tramoggia è crepata, o i chicchi sono oleosi e fanno ponte?*

> Very dark, oily beans: shake the hopper and fill it only halfway.

- Hopper cracked → **part, fitted by the customer**: MC-7040 (Hopper 1.2 kg polycarbonate)
- Oily beans, bridging → **fixed remotely**
