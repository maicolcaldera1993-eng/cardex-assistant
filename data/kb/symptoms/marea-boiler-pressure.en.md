# Boiler pressure too high or too low

*Pressione della caldaia troppo alta o troppo bassa*

Known-defects file, Marea family. Internal service document.

## How customers describe it {#descrizioni}

«pressione caldaia alta», «pressione caldaia bassa», «va sopra i due bar», «sfiata la valvola», «la lancetta della caldaia», «boiler pressure high», «boiler pressure low», «goes over 2 bar», «safety valve hisses», «boiler needle», «too much steam pressure», «troppa pressione in caldaia», «poca pressione in caldaia», «pressione troppo alta»

## Procedure {#procedura}

### 1. Ask: When hot, where does the boiler needle settle? Does it swing? {#passo-1}

*A macchina calda la lancetta della caldaia dove si ferma? Oscilla?*

> Normal 1.0-1.3 bar.

- Above 1.8 bar, safety valve vents → go to step 2
- Out of range but steady → go to step 3
- Swings a lot → **part, fitted by the customer**: CA-1241 (Pressurestat diaphragm), CA-1240 (Pressurestat 0.5–1.4 bar)

### 2. Have them do: Have them switch the machine off. When cold, remove the pressurestat capillary and blow it through; clean the fitting on the boiler. Switch on. {#passo-2}

*Far spegnere la macchina. A macchina fredda, staccare il capillare del pressostato e soffiarlo; pulire il raccordo sulla caldaia. Riaccendere.*

- Now regulates: fixed → **fixed remotely**
- Still climbs past 1.8 → **part, fitted by the customer**: CA-1240 (Pressurestat 0.5–1.4 bar)
- Vents even at 1.2 bar → **part, fitted by the customer**: CA-1250 (Safety valve 1.8 bar certified)

### 3. Have them do: Have them adjust the pressurestat screw (clockwise raises) to 1.1-1.2 bar. {#passo-3}

*Far regolare la vite del pressostato (senso orario alza) fino a 1,1-1,2 bar.*

- Holds the setting → **fixed remotely**
- Does not hold → **part, fitted by the customer**: CA-1240 (Pressurestat 0.5–1.4 bar)
