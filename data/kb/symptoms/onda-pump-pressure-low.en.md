# Low pump pressure, or noisy pump

*Pressione della pompa bassa, o pompa rumorosa*

Known-defects file, Onda family. Internal service document.

## How customers describe it {#descrizioni}

«pressione bassa», «la pompa non va in pressione», «pompa rumorosa», «la pompa ronza», «manometro basso», «low pressure», «pump pressure low», «noisy pump», «pump hums», «gauge is low»

## Procedure {#procedura}

### 1. Ask: During a shot, does the pump gauge read 9 bar or below 8? {#passo-1}

*Durante l'erogazione, il manometro della pompa segna 9 bar o meno di 8?*

- Below 8 bar → go to step 2
- 9 bar, normal → **technician**

### 2. Ask: Does the pump start and run, or hum without starting? {#passo-2}

*La pompa parte e gira, oppure ronza senza partire?*

- Hums, does not start → **part with service support**: ID-4019 (Pump motor capacitor 8 µF)
- Runs but pressure is low → go to step 3
- Runs with a grinding noise → go to step 4

### 3. Ask: When was the inlet filter cartridge last changed? {#passo-3}

*Quando è stata cambiata la cartuccia del filtro d'ingresso?*

> New filter and low pressure: the bypass valve (ID-4120) is adjusted with service on the line.

- More than three months ago → **part, fitted by the customer**: ID-4061 (Inlet filter cartridge (kit of 3))
- Recently → **part with service support**: ID-4120 (Rotary pump bypass valve (9 bar adjustment))

### 4. Ask: Which model is it: MB2, MB2 Evo or MB3? {#passo-4}

*Che modello è: MB2, MB2 Evo o MB3?*

- MB2 or MB2 Evo → **part with service support**: ID-4010 (Rotary pump 100 L/h 3/8" ports)
- MB3 → **part with service support**: ID-4011 (Rotary pump 150 L/h 3/8" ports, Onda MB3)
