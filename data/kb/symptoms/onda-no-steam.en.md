# No steam, or weak steam

*Niente vapore, o vapore debole*

Known-defects file, Onda family. Internal service document.

## How customers describe it {#descrizioni}

«niente vapore», «non fa vapore», «vapore debole», «poco vapore», «non monta il latte», «caldaia vapore fredda», «no steam», «weak steam», «not enough steam», «cannot steam milk», «steam boiler cold»

## Procedure {#procedura}

### 1. Ask: Is the steam boiler gauge at zero, or is it under pressure but the steam is weak? {#passo-1}

*Il manometro della caldaia vapore è a zero, o è in pressione ma il vapore è debole?*

- At zero, boiler cold → go to step 2
- Under pressure, weak steam → go to step 5

### 2. Ask: On the panel, is the steam boiler switched on (steam icon lit)? {#passo-2}

*Nel pannello, la caldaia vapore è attiva (icona vapore accesa)?*

- It was off: switched on, now heats → **fixed remotely**
- It is on but stays cold → go to step 3

### 3. Ask: Does the display show a pressure sensor error, or does the pressure stay at 0.0 with no error? {#passo-3}

*Sul display compare un errore sensore di pressione, o la pressione resta a 0,0 senza errori?*

- Sensor error → **part with service support**: CA-1245 (Boiler pressure transducer 0–3 bar)
- No error, stays at zero → go to step 4

### 4. Ask: Which model and voltage (rating plate)? {#passo-4}

*Che modello e che tensione (targhetta)?*

- MB2 or MB2 Evo, 230 V → **part with service support**: CA-1200 (Steam boiler heating element 3200 W 230 V, Onda MB2 / MB2 Evo), CA-1221 (Heating element flange gasket, Onda)
- MB2 or MB2 Evo, 110 V → **part with service support**: CA-1201 (Steam boiler heating element 3200 W 110 V, Onda MB2 / MB2 Evo), CA-1221 (Heating element flange gasket, Onda)
- MB3, 400 V three-phase → **part with service support**: CA-1202 (Steam boiler heating element 4800 W 400 V three-phase, Onda MB3), CA-1221 (Heating element flange gasket, Onda)
- MB3, 230 V → **part with service support**: CA-1203 (Steam boiler heating element 4800 W 230 V, Onda MB3), CA-1221 (Heating element flange gasket, Onda)

### 5. Have them do: Have them unscrew the steam tip: 30 minutes in hot water with a tablet, a pin through the holes, refit. {#passo-5}

*Far svitare il terminale della lancia: 30 minuti in acqua calda con una pastiglia, uno spillo nei fori, rimontare.*

- Fixed → **fixed remotely**
- Holes worn open → **part, fitted by the customer**: VA-5025 (Steam tip 2-hole)
