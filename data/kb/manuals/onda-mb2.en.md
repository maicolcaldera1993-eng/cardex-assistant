# Sereni Onda MB2 — User and maintenance manual

Sereni Macchine da Caffè S.r.l., Florence. 2024 edition, rev. 1. Keep with the machine.
Model: Onda MB2 (2 groups, multi-boiler, PID per group).

## 1. Description {#descrizione}
<!-- topics: what machine is this, how it works; com'è fatta la macchina, come funziona -->

The Onda MB2 is a professional machine for high-volume venues, up to 500 coffees a day. A steam boiler produces steam and hot water; each group has its own 0.8 L coffee boiler (CA-1325) with an 800 W element and a PT100 probe, and a heated jacket on the group. Temperature is set group by group on the panel. Rotary 100 l/h pump, 24 V solenoids.

A steam boiler plus one small coffee boiler per group, each with its own PID: coffee temperature set per group.

## 3. Installation {#installazione}
<!-- topics: installation, water connection, water hardness, softener, first start-up; installazione, allacciamento acqua, durezza dell'acqua, addolcitore, prima accensione -->

Authorised staff only. Mains water below 8 °f hardness, or a softener: **the warranty does not cover scale damage.** Check the rated voltage before connecting. At first start-up the steam boiler fills first, then the group boilers (about 6 minutes); each group is ready when its panel stops flashing.

## 4. Daily use {#uso-quotidiano}
<!-- topics: daily use, switching on, how to program the doses, buttons, steam, hot water; uso quotidiano, accensione, programmare le dosi, tasti, vapore, acqua calda -->

- **Switching on**: main switch on; ready in 25-30 minutes.
- **Brewing**: portafilter locked to the centre, dose button.
- **Programming doses**: hold the continuous button 5 seconds until it flashes, start the dose, press the same button at the desired amount.
- **Temperature**: on each group's panel, + and − buttons; recommended 93 °C.
- **Steam**: joystick up for full steam, down for pulsed steam; purge 2 seconds before and after.
- **Steam boiler**: can be switched off from the panel (steam icon) in quiet hours.
- **Hot water**: hot water button.

## 5. Routine maintenance {#manutenzione-ordinaria}
<!-- topics: routine maintenance, what the user must do, cleaning schedule; manutenzione ordinaria, pulizia, cosa deve fare l'utente -->

Everything below is the user's job. Nine service calls out of ten come from one of these being skipped.

### Every day, at closing time {#ogni-giorno-a-fine-servizio}
<!-- topics: daily cleaning, backflush, clean the groups with the blind filter and a tablet, how often to clean, detergent; pulizia giornaliera, lavaggio dei gruppi con filtro cieco e pastiglia, ogni quanto si lava -->
1. **Backflush the groups with the blind filter (GE-2193) and a tablet (CR-6052)**: one tablet in the blind filter, 5 cycles of 10 seconds with the continuous button, then 5 cycles without the tablet. On each group.
2. Portafilters and baskets in hot water with a tablet for 20 minutes, rinse.
3. Steam wand: purge and wipe; unscrew and rinse the tip (VA-5025) once a week.
4. Empty and wash the drip tray and grid (CR-6034).

### Every week {#ogni-settimana}
<!-- topics: weekly cleaning, brush the shower screen, check how the portafilter locks; pulizia settimanale, spazzolare la doccetta, aggancio del portafiltro -->
- Brush the shower screen with the group brush (CR-6051) without removing it.
- Check how the portafilter locks: if it goes past the centre, the gasket (GE-2410) needs replacing.

### Every month {#ogni-mese}
<!-- topics: monthly maintenance, remove and descale the shower screens, water filter cartridge; manutenzione mensile, smontare e decalcificare le doccette, cartuccia del filtro -->
- Remove the shower screens (centre screw) and leave them 20 minutes in descaler; replace if deformed (GE-2151).
- Check the inlet filter cartridge (ID-4061); replace it every 3 months, every month with hard water.

### Every 6-12 months {#ogni-6-12-mesi}
<!-- topics: replace the group gaskets, how often to change the gasket, steam valve seals; sostituire le guarnizioni sottocoppa, ogni quanto si cambia la guarnizione -->
- Replace the group gaskets (GE-2410) or fit the group service kit (GE-2211). A 10-minute job, see the part sheet.
- Replace the steam joystick seal kit (VA-5019) if it drips.

### Every 2 years, by the service network {#ogni-2-anni-a-cura-del-service}
<!-- topics: two-year service, safety valve, anti-vacuum valve, level probe, boiler descaling; revisione biennale, valvola di sicurezza, decalcificazione caldaia -->
- Safety valve (CA-1251), anti-vacuum valve (CA-1260), level probe (CA-1231), descaling of the boilers.

## 6. Lights and signals {#spie-e-segnali}
<!-- topics: warning lights, flashing LEDs, alarms, level alarm, error codes, what does the blinking mean; spie, led che lampeggiano, allarmi, allarme livello, errori sul pannello -->

| Signal | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| All panels flashing | steam boiler level alarm | check the water tap; if it persists, call service |
| PRB on a group panel | temperature probe error | call service |
| Group temperature not rising | group boiler not heating or safety thermostat tripped | call service |
| Steam pressure 0.0 with the icon lit | steam boiler not heating | call service |
| Venting from the safety valve | pressure over the limit | switch off, call service |

## 7. What not to do {#cosa-non-fare}
<!-- topics: what not to do, warnings, never adjust the safety valve, do not run without water; cosa non fare, avvertenze -->

- Do not run the machine with the water off.
- Do not adjust the safety valve.
- Do not use any detergent other than CR-6052 tablets in the groups.
- Do not replace elements, pump or control board without service on the line.
- Do not leave the steam wand in the milk.
