# Sereni Marea 2 Plus — User and maintenance manual

Sereni Macchine da Caffè S.r.l., Florence. 2025 edition, rev. 2. Keep with the machine.
Model: Marea 2 Plus (2 groups, heat exchanger, volumetric dosing, PID control). Also available in the Vaniglia edition (cream finish).

## 1. Description {#descrizione}

The Marea 2 Plus is a two-group professional machine for bars and restaurants serving up to 400 coffees a day. An 11-litre copper boiler produces steam and hot water; brew water passes through two heat exchangers immersed in the boiler and reaches the groups at 92-94 °C. A PID controller on the exchanger stabilises the brew temperature; a pressurestat keeps the boiler at 1.1-1.2 bar. Dosing is volumetric: four programmable doses per group on a six-button capacitive touchpad.

Compared with the Marea 2 (2024): capacitive touchpad, v2 control board, PID on the exchanger, hot-water solenoid with mixing valve. The Marea 2 Evo (2026) has an insulated boiler, a display and a v3 board: its manual is different.

## 3. Installation {#installazione}

Authorised personnel only. Mains water below 8 °f hardness or a softener: **the warranty does not cover scale damage.** Check the rating plate voltage before connecting. At first start-up the boiler fills automatically (about 4 minutes); wait until the boiler needle reaches 1.1 bar before brewing. A hiss from the anti-vacuum valve in the first minutes is normal.

## 4. Daily use {#uso-quotidiano}

- **Switching on**: main switch to position 1 (water fill only), then 2 (heating). Ready in 20-25 minutes.
- **Brewing**: portafilter locked at the centre, dose button. Buttons: 1 short, 1 long, 2 short, 2 long, continuous, stop.
- **Programming doses**: hold the continuous button for 5 seconds until the LEDs flash, start the dose, press the same button at the desired amount. Repeat for each button and each group.
- **Steam**: open the tap, purge for 2 seconds, dip the wand. After use purge and wipe with a damp cloth.
- **Hot water**: hot-water button, temperature adjustable on the mixing valve.

## 5. Routine maintenance {#manutenzione-ordinaria}

Everything below is the user's responsibility. Nine service calls out of ten start with one of these steps being skipped.

### Every day, at closing time {#ogni-giorno-a-fine-servizio}
1. **Backflush the groups with the blind filter (GE-2193) and a tablet (CR-6052)**: one tablet in the blind filter, 5 cycles of 10 seconds with the continuous button, then 5 cycles without the tablet. Each group.
2. Portafilters and baskets in hot water with a tablet for 20 minutes, rinse.
3. Steam wand: purge, wipe, and once a week unscrew and rinse the tip (VA-5025).
4. Empty and wash the drip tray and grid.

### Every week {#ogni-settimana}
- Brush the shower screen with the group brush (CR-6051) without removing it.
- Check how the portafilter locks: if it goes past the centre, the gasket is due.

### Every month {#ogni-mese}
- Remove the shower screens (centre screw) and leave them 20 minutes in descaler; replace if domed (GE-2150).
- Check the inlet filter cartridge (ID-4061); replace every 3 months, monthly with hard water.

### Every 6-12 months {#ogni-6-12-mesi}
- Replace the group gaskets (GE-2140) or fit the group kit (GE-2210). A 10-minute job, see the part sheet.
- Replace the steam valve seal kit (VA-5015) if the taps drip.

### Every 2 years, by the service network {#ogni-2-anni-a-cura-del-service}
- Safety valve (CA-1250), anti-vacuum valve (CA-1260), level probe (CA-1230), hydraulic service kit (ID-4100), boiler descaling.

## 6. Lights and signals {#spie-e-segnali}

| Signal | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| All button LEDs flash together | level alarm: the boiler did not fill within 3 minutes | check the water tap; switch off and on; if it persists, call the service desk |
| A button LED flashes during the dose | flowmeter alarm: the counter receives no pulses | brew in continuous mode; if the dose does not stop, service desk |
| Short hiss at start-up | anti-vacuum valve | normal if it stops within 5 minutes |
| Safety valve venting | pressure above 1.8 bar | switch off, call the service desk |
| Pump gauge below 8 bar while brewing | low pressure | see the service desk |

## 7. What not to do {#cosa-non-fare}

- Never run the machine with the water supply closed.
- Never adjust the safety valve.
- Use only CR-6052 tablets in the groups.
- Never replace the heating element, the pump or the control board without the service desk on the line.
- Never leave the steam wand in the milk.
