# Sereni Monda 65 — User and maintenance manual

Sereni Macchine da Caffè S.r.l., Florence. 2024 edition, rev. 1. Keep with the grinder.
Model: Monda 65 (professional grinder, 65 mm flat burrs).

## 1. Description {#descrizione}
<!-- topics: what grinder is this, how it works; com'è fatto il macinacaffè, come funziona -->

Bar grinder with 65 mm hardened steel flat burrs (MC-7010), 350 W motor, 1.2 kg hopper (MC-7040). Dosing: chamber doser with six-sector star and lever (MC-7030).

The doser grinder: it grinds into the chamber and doses with the lever, one star sector per dose.

## 3. Installation {#installazione}
<!-- topics: installation, connection, first start-up; installazione, collegamento, primo avvio -->

Place on a stable counter on the anti-vibration feet (MC-7080). Check the rated voltage. At first start-up grind and discard 200 g of coffee to bed in the burrs.

## 4. Daily use {#uso-quotidiano}
<!-- topics: daily use, how to adjust the grind, dosing; uso quotidiano, regolare la macinatura, dosare -->

- **Grind adjustment**: collar (MC-7075), clockwise for finer. Move one notch at a time, with the motor running.
- **Dosing**: grind into the chamber, then one pull of the lever per dose; do not keep more than ten doses in the chamber.

## 5. Routine maintenance {#manutenzione-ordinaria}
<!-- topics: routine maintenance, what the user must do, cleaning schedule; manutenzione ordinaria, pulizia, cosa deve fare l'utente -->

### Every day, at closing time {#ogni-giorno-a-fine-servizio}
<!-- topics: daily cleaning; pulizia giornaliera -->
- Empty the doser and brush clean, no water.

### Every week {#ogni-settimana}
<!-- topics: weekly cleaning, hopper; pulizia settimanale, tramoggia -->
- Empty and wash the hopper (lukewarm water, dry well).

### Every month {#ogni-mese}
<!-- topics: clean the burrs; pulizia delle macine -->
- Close the shutter, unscrew the collar and brush the burrs and the chamber clean.

### Every 12 months or 800 kg {#ogni-12-mesi-o-800-kg}
<!-- topics: replace the burrs, how often to change the burrs; sostituire le macine, ogni quanto si cambiano le macine -->
- Replace the burrs (MC-7010) and screws (MC-7011). A 20-minute job, see the part sheet.

## 6. Lights and signals {#spie-e-segnali}
<!-- topics: warning lights, noises, alarms; spie, rumori, allarmi -->

| Signal | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Motor hums and does not turn | burrs jammed by a foreign object | switch off and clear the chamber |
| Burning smell | motor overheating | switch off, call service |
| Metallic noise | loose burr screws or chipped burrs | stop it at once |

## 7. What not to do {#cosa-non-fare}
<!-- topics: what not to do, warnings; cosa non fare, avvertenze -->

- Do not wash the burrs with water.
- Do not turn the collar with the motor off and coffee in the chamber.
- Do not grind caramelised or flavoured beans.
